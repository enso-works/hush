The hush dashboard on your iPhone and iPad. hush is in-app feedback and anonymous usage tracking for mobile and web apps, on a server you run. This app reads that server, so you can answer feedback and check your numbers away from your desk.

Feedback
- Read, answer, close, reopen and delete feedback, with filters, search and quick replies.
- A notification when new feedback or a reply arrives; answer or close it from the notification.

Each app's numbers
- The Overview: installs, active users and open feedback for every app on the server.
- An app's page: active installs, sessions, new installs and how they compare with the month before.
- Funnels, and a funnel builder from your own events.
- Retention and weekly cohorts, engagement, audience (versions, countries, build channels) and every event with its properties.
- Look up a single install by its id.

Remote config
- Edit keys and their rules, see what a device gets before you change anything, and revert from the history.

Servers and phones
- Add a server by scanning the QR code on your dashboard's Phones page, or by hand. Each phone gets its own token, which you can revoke.
- Not running a server yet? Try the live demo.

Privacy
hush counts installs, not people. A ticket with an email is never linked to an install. Your server's data stays on your server and on this device; the app only sends its own anonymous usage, and you can turn that off in Settings.
